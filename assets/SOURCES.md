# Style reference

`pan_tadeusz_excerpt.txt`: Adam Mickiewicz, *Pan Tadeusz* (1834), Book I. Public-domain literary text, from the Wolne Lektury edition already used in Falenty.

Source: https://wolnelektury.pl/katalog/lektura/pan-tadeusz.html

Wolne Lektury credits its source edition as *Pisma Adama Mickiewicza*, volume V, Paris 1860; digital reproduction by Biblioteka Narodowa; editorial work by Maria Bailey, Anna Dyja, Marta Niedziałkowska, Agnieszka Paul, Magdalena Paul, Aleksandra Sekuła and Weronika Trzeciak. This short excerpt contains the literary text, without editorial notes.

The witty-dialogue prompt examples and all user questions in `style_data.py` are original workshop material. No film script or film quotations are included. Generated training answers record their teacher model and revision in the dataset manifest.

The later full-book experiment separately includes the complete Wolne Lektury download in `datasets/pan-tadeusz-full/source.txt`, the training text in `train.txt`, and the original source credits in `SOURCE_CREDITS.txt`. See `PAN_TADEUSZ.md` for preprocessing and provenance. This corpus was not used by the earlier 64-example persona adapters.
