Why does the Moon have phases?

Before · ordinary question

The Moon has phases because of **the changing angles at which we view the sunlit portion of the Moon as it orbits the Earth.**

Here is a detailed breakdown of the process:

---

### 1. The Moon Does Not Produce Its Own Light

The most crucial point to understand is that **the Moon does not produce its own light.** It is a natural satellite that reflects sunlight. The phases we observe are simply different amounts of the Moon's sunlit side that are facing Earth.

### 2. The Moon Orbits the Earth

The Moon is constantly orbiting the Earth. As it orbits, the angle between the **Sun, the Earth, and the Moon** constantly changes.

### 3. Sunlight Illuminates the Moon

The Sun provides the light. As the Moon moves around the Earth, different parts of the Moon are illuminated by the Sun at any given time.
